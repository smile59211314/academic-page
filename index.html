Profile

I work at the intersection of electromagnetics, machine dynamics, and intelligent control—from magnetic equivalent circuits to physics-informed Koopman operators.

This research spans eccentricity and bearing fault modelling, multiphysics coupling, reduced-order system identification, and sensor fault-tolerant control for electrical machines and power electronic systems.
